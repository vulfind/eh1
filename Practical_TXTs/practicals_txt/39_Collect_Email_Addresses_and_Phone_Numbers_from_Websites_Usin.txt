Practical No. 39

Collect Email Addresses and Phone Numbers from Websites Using
OSINT Tools
Aim: To gather publicly exposed email addresses and phone numbers of a target organisation using OSINT techniques.
Tools / Requirements: Kali Linux, theHarvester, Hunter.io (web), Google Dorking
Procedure:
  1.   Run theHarvester for emails: theHarvester -d example.com -b all
  2.   Use Hunter.io in a browser: search the target domain to list discovered email addresses and their sources.
  3.   Use Google dorking for contact pages: site:example.com "contact" intext:"@example.com"
  4.   Search for phone numbers using dorks: site:example.com intext:"+91" OR intext:"phone"
  5.   Cross-verify and compile a consolidated list of discovered emails and phone numbers for the report.
