Practical No. 43

Identify Open Ports and Services on Metasploitable Using Nmap
Aim: To enumerate open ports, services and versions on a Metasploitable VM.
Tools / Requirements: Kali Linux, Nmap, Metasploitable 2/3 VM
Procedure:
  1.   Full version scan: nmap -sV -p- <metasploitable-ip>
  2.   Add default scripts for extra detail: nmap -sC -sV <metasploitable-ip>
  3.   Expected notable open ports include: 21 (FTP – vsftpd, often backdoored), 22 (SSH), 23 (Telnet), 25 (SMTP), 80
       (HTTP), 139/445 (SMB), 1099 (Java-RMI), 3306 (MySQL), 5432 (PostgreSQL), 6667 (IRC – UnrealIRCd
       backdoor), 8180 (Tomcat).
  4.   Tabulate each open port with its service, version, and any known associated vulnerability for follow-on exploitation
       practicals.
