Practical No. 21

Google Dorking to Find Hidden Files and Directories
Aim: To use advanced Google search operators (dorks) for passive reconnaissance of a target's exposed files/directories.
Tools / Requirements: Web browser, Google Search
Procedure:
  1.   Restrict to a site and file type: site:example.com filetype:pdf
  2.   Find open directory listings: intitle:"index of" "parent directory" site:example.com
  3.   Find admin panels: inurl:admin site:example.com
  4.   Find sensitive leaked files: site:example.com ext:sql OR ext:env OR ext:log
  5.   Find exposed credentials/spreadsheets: intext:"password" filetype:xls site:example.com
  6.   Document every exposed resource found, and note this is passive, authorised-scope reconnaissance only.
