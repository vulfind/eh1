Practical No. 12

Social Media Intelligence Gathering Using Maltego
Aim: To gather OSINT on a target (person/domain/organisation) using Maltego transforms.
Tools / Requirements: Kali Linux, Maltego (Community Edition)
Procedure:
  1.   Launch Maltego and register/login with a free Community Edition (CE) account.
  2.   Start a new graph and drag a Domain or Person entity onto the canvas.
  3.   Enter the target value (e.g. example.com or a name).
  4.   Right-click the entity → Run Transform → choose transforms such as 'To Email Address', 'To Social Network
       Profiles', 'To Phone Numbers' (from the installed transform hub).
  5.   Review the resulting graph of linked emails, social media profiles, domains and infrastructure.
  6.   Export the graph/report for documentation.
