Practical No. 7

theHarvester – Email and Subdomain Enumeration
Aim: To collect email addresses and subdomains of a target organisation using OSINT.
Tools / Requirements: Kali Linux, theHarvester
Procedure:
  1.   Run: theHarvester -d example.com -b google
  2.   Use multiple sources at once: theHarvester -d example.com -b all
  3.   Review the discovered email addresses, subdomains, hostnames and IP addresses in the output.
  4.   Save results to a file: theHarvester -d example.com -b all -f results
  5.   Document findings for the reconnaissance report.
