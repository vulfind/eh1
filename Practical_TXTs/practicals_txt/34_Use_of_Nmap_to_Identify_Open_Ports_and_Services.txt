Practical No. 34

Use of Nmap to Identify Open Ports and Services
Aim: To identify all open ports and the services running on them for a given target using Nmap.
Tools / Requirements: Kali Linux, Nmap, target VM
Procedure:
  1.   Quick scan of common ports: nmap <target-ip>
  2.   Full port range: nmap -p- <target-ip>
  3.   Service/version detection: nmap -sV <target-ip>
  4.   Combine with default scripts: nmap -sC -sV -p- <target-ip>
  5.   Tabulate the port number, protocol, state and detected service/version for the report.
